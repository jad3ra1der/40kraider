# Warhammer Showcase
This project is to create a portfolio website of sorts showcasing one of my most recent hobbies, my Warhammer 40K army.

## What is this?
It is a list of all of the unique units in my army, the Emperor's Children, as well as a brief overview of their in-game lore and purpose, pictures of my miniatures as well as their official art and painted mini, and their in-game stats and data.

## Why does it exist?
I have worked very hard on this army and I wanted a way to easily share photos of my builds along with small excerpts about the characters themselves with others.

## What tools did I use?
VS Code only.
